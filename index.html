OwnLock

Lock distracting apps behind a real-world key.

OwnLock is a screen-time management app for iPhone. You choose which apps to block, set a minimum lock time, and unlock only by physically tapping a paired NFC tag or scanning a paired QR code. The friction is the feature — it turns unlocking into a deliberate choice instead of an impulse.

How it works

  1. Create a blocking profile (pick apps, set a minimum lock time, choose strict mode if you want)
  2. Pair an NFC tag or QR code as the unlock key
  3. Lock the profile. The chosen apps are shielded by Apple's Family Controls framework until you tap the tag.

Privacy

OwnLock has no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and no server. All your profiles, tag pairings, and unlock history live on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Screen Time authorization is handled entirely by Apple's Family Controls framework. We never see what apps you block or when you unlock.
