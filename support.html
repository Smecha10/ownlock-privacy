# OwnLock Support

**Lock distracting apps behind a real-world key.** Pair an NFC tag or QR code, lock the apps that steal your attention, and unlocking takes a deliberate physical tap.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I unlock my apps?**
Tap your paired NFC tag (hold your iPhone near it) or scan your paired QR code. The unlock action is physical by design — it makes distraction a deliberate choice.

**What is Strict Mode?**
Strict Mode enforces the minimum lock time you set. While a lock is active, you can't unlock early even with the tag — useful when willpower runs low.

**I lost my NFC tag. Can I get back into my apps?**
Yes. If you set a minimum lock time, the lock expires naturally when the time runs out. Otherwise, contact support below and we'll help you regain access.

**Why does the app ask for Screen Time access?**
Apple requires permission for any app that manages device app usage. Granting it lets OwnLock block the apps in your profiles. We never see or store what you use.

**Does OwnLock work on iPad?**
OwnLock is designed for iPhone. iPad support is on the roadmap.
