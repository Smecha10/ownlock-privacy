# OwnLock Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

OwnLock (the "App") is a screen-time management app developed by Spencer Mecham ("Developer," "we," "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and what we collect — which is nothing.

## The short version

**We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data.** The App has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no server.

## What the App stores (on your device only)

- **Blocking profiles and settings** — stored locally on your device.
- **NFC tag / QR code pairings** — stored locally on your device.
- **Screen Time authorization** — handled by Apple's Family Controls framework; we never see this data.

All data stays on your device. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

## App Store purchase data

Apple processes all payments. We never receive your name, payment details, or contact information from Apple.

## Children

OwnLock contains no content inappropriate for children and collects no data from any user, child or adult.

## Changes

If we ever change these practices, we will update this page and the App's privacy label before the change ships.
